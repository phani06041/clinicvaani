# Navjeevan General Insurance — Family Floater Policy (fictional demo data)

## NAV-FF 1.3 Network status
Arogya Family Clinic is NOT a network provider for Navjeevan. All treatment there is on a reimbursement basis: the patient pays and submits the claim within 30 days with original bills, prescriptions and reports.

## NAV-FF 3.2 OPD consultations
OPD consultations are reimbursed up to Rs 500 per visit, maximum 4 visits per policy year for the whole family.

## NAV-FF 5.1 Cataract surgery
Covered after a 24 month waiting period. Sub-limit Rs 30,000 per eye. Prior intimation to Navjeevan 72 hours before planned surgery is required for reimbursement.

## NAV-FF 5.3 Knee replacement
Covered after a 36 month waiting period. Sub-limit Rs 1,80,000 per knee.

## NAV-FF 6.2 Maternity
Covered after 24 months. Rs 35,000 for normal delivery and Rs 50,000 for C-section. New-born baby covered from day one up to Rs 25,000.

## NAV-FF 8.1 Exclusions
Physiotherapy on OPD basis, spectacles, cosmetic and dental treatment are excluded.
