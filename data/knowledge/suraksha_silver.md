# Suraksha Health — Silver Plan Policy Wording (fictional demo data)

## SUR-S 1.2 Cashless network
Cashless treatment is available at network providers, including Arogya Family Clinic, Madanapalli.

## SUR-S 3.1 OPD consultations
Out-patient consultations are NOT covered under the Silver plan. Only in-patient hospitalisation and listed day-care procedures are covered.

## SUR-S 5.2 Cataract surgery
Cataract surgery is covered after a waiting period of 24 months. Sub-limit Rs 25,000 per eye. 10 percent co-payment applies on every claim. Pre-authorization required 48 hours in advance.

## SUR-S 5.4 Knee replacement
Covered after a 48 month waiting period. Sub-limit Rs 1,50,000 per knee. 10 percent co-payment. Pre-authorization mandatory.

## SUR-S 6.1 Maternity
Maternity is not covered under the Silver plan.

## SUR-S 7.4 Exclusions
OPD diagnostics, OPD physiotherapy, cosmetic procedures, spectacles and dental treatment are not covered.
