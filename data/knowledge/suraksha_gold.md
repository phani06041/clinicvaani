# Suraksha Health — Gold Plan Policy Wording (fictional demo data)

## SUR-G 1.2 Cashless network
Cashless treatment is available at network providers, including Arogya Family Clinic, Madanapalli. The member must show the Suraksha e-card and a photo ID.

## SUR-G 3.1 OPD consultations
Out-patient doctor consultations are covered cashless up to Rs 1,000 per visit, maximum 6 visits per policy year. No pre-authorization needed.

## SUR-G 3.3 OPD diagnostics
Out-patient diagnostic tests (blood tests, X-ray, ECG, ultrasound) prescribed by a registered doctor are covered up to Rs 3,000 per policy year.

## SUR-G 5.2 Cataract surgery
Cataract surgery is covered after a waiting period of 24 months from the first policy start date. Sub-limit Rs 40,000 per eye. Pre-authorization must be requested at least 48 hours before a planned surgery. No co-payment.

## SUR-G 5.4 Knee replacement
Joint replacement surgery is covered after a waiting period of 48 months. Sub-limit Rs 2,00,000 per knee. Pre-authorization mandatory.

## SUR-G 6.1 Maternity
Maternity expenses are covered after 36 months of continuous coverage. Sub-limit Rs 50,000 for normal delivery and Rs 75,000 for C-section. Pre-natal OPD visits count under SUR-G 3.1.

## SUR-G 7.4 Exclusions
Out-patient physiotherapy, cosmetic procedures, spectacles and contact lenses, and dental treatment (except due to accident) are not covered.

## SUR-G 9.1 Pre-authorization turnaround
Pre-authorization requests are decided within 6 working hours of receiving complete documents: doctor's recommendation, estimate, ID, e-card and relevant reports.
