# Arogya Family Clinic — Front Desk Handbook (fictional demo data)

## Timings and location
Arogya Family Clinic, Main Road, Madanapalli, Andhra Pradesh (fictional address). Open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Closed on Sundays and national holidays. The lab opens at 7:30 AM for fasting blood tests.

## Consultation fees
General physician consultation is Rs 400. Specialist consultation is Rs 500 to Rs 700 depending on the doctor. A follow-up visit within 7 days with the same doctor is free. Senior citizens above 65 get 20 percent off consultation fees. Payment by UPI, card or cash.

## Appointments, tokens and walk-ins
Appointments can be booked by phone, WhatsApp or at the counter. Every confirmed appointment gets an appointment ID and an SMS. Please arrive 15 minutes early for registration. Walk-ins are accepted but booked patients are seen first. Late arrival of more than 20 minutes may need a new slot.

## Cancellation and rescheduling
Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 2 hours before the slot. No-shows twice in a row lose priority booking for 30 days.

## What to bring
Bring a government photo ID, previous prescriptions and reports, the insurance card or policy number, and the list of current medicines. For children, bring the vaccination card.

## Reports and lab results
Routine blood test reports are ready the same evening after 6 PM and are sent on WhatsApp. Culture reports take 48 to 72 hours. X-ray reports are ready in 2 hours.

## Accessibility and parking
Wheelchair ramp and lift available. Free two-wheeler parking in front; car parking in the lane behind the clinic. A staff member can help elderly patients from the auto stand on request.

## Emergencies
The clinic is not an emergency hospital. For chest pain, breathing difficulty, unconsciousness, seizures, heavy bleeding or stroke signs, call 108 ambulance immediately or go to the nearest government hospital emergency department.

## Insurance and cashless
The clinic is a cashless network provider for Suraksha Health (all plans). Navjeevan General Insurance is NOT cashless at this clinic; patients pay and claim reimbursement, and the front desk gives an itemised bill and discharge summary for the claim.
